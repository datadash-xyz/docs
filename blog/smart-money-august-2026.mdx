August was a quiet month by volume and a loud one by concentration. Total traded volume across tracked markets fell 12% from July, but the top 100 wallets by PnL took a larger share of it than in any month since we started measuring — 31%, against a 2026 average of 22%.

Fewer traders, less volume, and considerably more money made by the wallets we already track. That combination usually means the informed side of the book got a cheaper month, not a better one.

## Where the edge was

We ran the **Crypto Whales** cohort against category performance for the month. Politics stayed the largest category by volume, but it was not where the returns were.

| Category | Cohort volume | Realized PnL | ROI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sports | $71.2M | $8.10M | 11.4% |
| Politics | $148.9M | $4.30M | 2.9% |
| Crypto | $52.4M | $3.60M | 6.9% |
| Economics | $19.8M | $1.90M | 9.6% |
| Culture | $12.1M | $1.00M | 8.3% |

Sports did roughly half the volume of Politics and nearly twice the PnL. The spread is the interesting part: Politics markets stayed liquid and efficiently priced through the month, while Sports repeatedly gave up 8–12 point mispricings in the last 48 hours before resolution.

## Signals that paid

The five highest-scoring positions opened in August, and how they resolved:

| Market | Score | Entry | Size | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Will the Q3 print exceed 2.4%? | 94 | $0.31 | $412K | Resolved Yes |
| City to win the league | 91 | $0.44 | $288K | Resolved Yes |
| Chair replaced before October | 88 | $0.19 | $190K | Resolved No |
| Merger cleared by regulators | 86 | $0.62 | $355K | Resolved Yes |
| Storm makes landfall as Cat 3+ | 84 | $0.27 | $145K | Resolved Yes |

Four of five resolved in the direction held. The one that didn't — the chair replacement market — was also the smallest and the earliest, entered 41 days before resolution against a median of 9 days for the rest.

That gap matches what the [signal score](/signals) already weights: conviction shown close to resolution, in size, at a price the trader had to move to get, is worth more than the same conviction expressed early and cheaply.

## Holder churn

Across the 20 largest events by open interest, roughly a third of positions turned over inside the month:

- **34%** of wallets holding on 1 August had fully exited by 31 August
- **19%** of end-of-month positions belonged to wallets with no prior fill in the event
- Median position age at exit: **6.2 days**, up from 4.8 in July

Longer holds with heavier churn at the edges is the pattern of a market where the core position-takers stayed put and the tourists rotated.

## Reproducing this

Every number above comes from three endpoints and a cohort:

```json
{
  "page": { "limit": 100 },
  "orderBy": [{ "field": "totalPnl", "direction": "desc" }],
  "filter": [
    { "field": "userId", "operator": "inCohort", "value": ["0J9XQ4M7B2K5T"] },
    { "field": "timestamp", "operator": "between",
      "value": { "from": "2026-08-01", "to": "2026-08-31" } }
  ]
}
```

Send that to `/api/v1/profiles/user-tag` for the category table, `/api/v1/signals/scores` for the signal table, and `/api/v1/market-holders/all` for the churn figures. See [Filtering](/filters) for the full grammar and [Generating a Client](/openapi-clients) if you'd rather do this in TypeScript or Python.
